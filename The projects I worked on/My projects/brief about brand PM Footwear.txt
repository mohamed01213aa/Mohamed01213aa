PM Footwear – Brand Brief
Brand Overview

PM Footwear is a premium Egyptian footwear brand specializing in handcrafted men's leather shoes made from 100% genuine cow leather. The brand focuses on delivering a balance between elegance, comfort, and durability through modern designs and high-quality craftsmanship.

The brand targets men who appreciate premium fashion, timeless style, and everyday comfort while offering a local Egyptian product that competes with international footwear brands. PM Footwear emphasizes quality materials, handcrafted production, secure online shopping, fast nationwide delivery, and a seamless customer experience.

Target Audience
Men aged approximately 22–50 years.
Middle to upper-class customers.
Professionals, business owners, and fashion-conscious customers.
Customers looking for premium genuine leather footwear suitable for work, casual wear, and formal occasions.
Brand Positioning

PM Footwear positions itself as a premium Egyptian leather footwear brand that combines:

Premium Quality
Modern Design
Everyday Comfort
Handcrafted Manufacturing
Affordable Luxury
